## About Me

I am pursuing a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering at Daffodil International University. My interests lie in artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, and medical image analysis.

I am developing my foundations in programming, mathematical reasoning, ML experimentation, and research methodology. I am particularly interested in making machine-learning predictions more reliable when images vary in quality or come from unfamiliar datasets. My longer-term goal is to pursue research-focused graduate studies and contribute to AI/ML research.

Alongside my studies, I build Android applications and coursework software projects, explore AI agents, and participate in programming and AI competitions.

## Current Research

**Quality- and Shift-Aware Selective Retinal Disease Classification Across Bangladeshi Clinical Datasets**

Undergraduate thesis · Research in progress · 2026–2027  
Supervisor: **Tanzina Afroz Rimi**, Daffodil International University

I am exploring how image quality and dataset shift affect retinal disease classification, how predictions generalize across clinical datasets, and whether selective classification can help identify unreliable predictions. The study is still being developed.
