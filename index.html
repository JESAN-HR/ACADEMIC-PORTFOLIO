Introduction

About Me

I am a Computer Science and Engineering undergraduate at Daffodil International University, Bangladesh, interested in artificial intelligence, machine learning, computer vision, and medical image analysis. I am developing my foundations in programming, mathematical reasoning, ML experimentation, and research methodology.

My current interests focus on making machine-learning systems more reliable and robust, particularly when applied to medical images and unfamiliar real-world datasets. My longer-term goal is to pursue research-focused graduate studies and contribute to AI/ML research.

Alongside my studies, I work on independent programming and coursework projects, including Android applications, and participate in AI competitions.

Research in Progress

2026 – 2027

Quality- and Shift-Aware Selective Retinal Disease Classification Across Bangladeshi Clinical Datasets

Undergraduate thesis · Research in progress

Supervisor: Tanzina Afroz Rimi, Daffodil International University

An ongoing study of retinal fundus image classification, image quality, and prediction reliability under distribution shift. The research is being developed to examine how models generalize across clinical datasets and identify unreliable predictions.

Research questions
  • How does image quality affect prediction reliability?
  • How well do models generalize across datasets?
  • Can selective classification help identify unreliable predictions?
